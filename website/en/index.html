KSeF invoices & bank transfers

Everything you need for KSeF invoices

From downloading an invoice, through the payment file, to confirming payment from your bank statement.

  • Purchase invoices from KSeF

    Download invoices and track statuses (new, disputed, to pay, transfer prepared, paid), with notes and history. Swipe gestures, date filters — this month, last month, custom range — export to Excel and PDF, and a cost summary.

  • Bank transfers

    A payment file for your bank from approved invoices — Erste Bank, mBank or Elixir-0 for other banks, including split payment (MPP). History of payment batches.

  • Bank statements

    Import MT940 or CSV (Erste, VeloBank) and payments are matched to invoices automatically. Approving the match marks the invoices as paid.

  • Sales invoices

    Issue FA(3) invoices, send them to KSeF, get the UPO receipt and the KSeF QR code. Buyer details by tax ID (NIP) from the VAT White List, plus a catalogue of items and buyers.

  • PDF and custom templates

    PDF invoice visualisation with your own layout, colour, logo and label language. Arrange the page by dragging sections.

  • Copy in iCloud Drive

    A copy in your own iCloud Drive as plain files: invoices as JSON and XML, payment files and statements.

  • Security

    Data stays on your phone, the KSeF token is kept in the Keychain, Face ID lock.

  • Payment reminders

    Due-date reminders go to the Reminders app and are checked off automatically once the invoice is paid.

  • Four languages

    Polish, English, Spanish and Ukrainian. Requires iOS 26.

Privacy at a glance

Your invoices stay with you — on your phone and in your own iCloud.

  • No accounts, no developer serverThe app does not collect personal data.
  • Data only on your deviceProtected by iOS file encryption; optional copy in your private iCloud.
  • KSeF token in the KeychainNever included in backups or iCloud.
  • No ads, analytics or trackingThis website uses no cookies or analytics tools either.
